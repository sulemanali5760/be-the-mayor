# Roadmap

Vision and pillars: [VISION.md](VISION.md). A milestone ships only its row; new ideas go into the backlog.

| Milestone | Theme | Contents | Ends when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.1** | First 15 minutes | Name yourself and the town. **Town view** (grey, run-down Brookfield). **Job board** with 2 named offers: a garden wall (first person, reusing the Brick by Brick models, ≤ 90 s) and a delivery route drawn on the map (45 s). Energy per day. **Problem board** with named citizens and trade-off choices, effects shown. About 15 **event cards** with humour and 1–2 delayed consequences. **Self-upgrade**: night school → electrician, a second-hand computer. **Career ladder** on screen. **Newspaper**. **Visits**: snapshot upload, a list of towns, a read-only visit, like, preset sign, help stamp (Supabase). A shareable **promotion card**. | A new player reaches **Skilled** in about 15 min and sees the council by-election hook (GAMES §5.1). |
| 0.2 | Community and council | 4–6 citizen groups with moods. Skilled first-person fixes: street light wiring, pothole patch. A by-election with signatures, a canvassing first-person task and a speech of promises. Council votes, allies, a named rival. Delegating mastered tasks. | You win (or lose, as a story) a council seat. |
| 0.3 | Mayor | Town budget with the REAL-WORLD §8.3 lines. 4–6 policy levers. Rule-of-the-day permit desk. Build and upgrade town buildings. Surprise events (storm, strike, scandal). Mayoral election and re-election. Player help requests. | You run the town for a term and face re-election. |
| 0.4 | Region | Governor or minister: several towns, inspecting other players' towns as part of the job, regional crises, a weekly town fair with a like leaderboard. | You reach the minister rank. |
| 0.5 | President | National crises as card chains, a legacy card, a seasonal presidency title across all towns, a new-term (prestige) loop. | The full arc is playable. |
| 1.0 | Public launch | Balance from play data, phone polish, onboarding, a report tool, performance. | Public link shared. |
